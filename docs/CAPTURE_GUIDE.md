# Capture Guide

## Good Capture

- Use diffuse light.
- Keep the object still.
- Move slowly around the object or use a turntable.
- Capture 24 to 80 sharp viewpoints.
- Include a known measurement: ruler, calibration block, marker board, or object dimension.
- Avoid motion blur.

## Hard Objects

- Reflective metal.
- Glossy plastic.
- Clear glass or acrylic.
- Deep black surfaces.
- Textureless white objects.
- Hair, cloth fuzz, chains, wires, and thin holes.

## For 3D Printing

1. Capture the object.
2. Export PLY or OBJ for review.
3. Export STL for slicer cleanup.
4. Use Blender, MeshLab, or Kai CAD repair to make the mesh watertight.
5. Rebuild mechanical faces and holes as CAD primitives when tolerance matters.
